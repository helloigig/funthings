Inside the experiment

A body made of tetrahedra

The thick walls, gusseted base and rolled rim are one layer of hexahedra split into ~2,000 tetrahedra; each handle is a separate chain tied into the rim. Edges carry XPBD distance constraints, every tetrahedron a volume constraint, so the jelly stretches but keeps its bulk.

Puff, damping, ground

A one-sided pressure constraint on the cavity stops the bag folding flat. Edge-wise damping only removes relative motion, so it wobbles and then settles without losing its swing. Fixed 1/120 s steps, six substeps each, at most three per frame. Rim and handles carry stiffer constraints.

Skin, glitter, metal

The visible surfaces, flecks, bubbles, rings and rivets are all embedded in the deforming cells with trilinear weights, so they ride along with every squish.

Light

WGSL passes accumulate signed path length through the jelly from the camera and from the key light. That drives Beer–Lambert absorption, the tinted soft shadow and the scattered glow; a depth-peeled second layer shows the inside back wall through a refracted, dispersed view, under Fresnel reflections of the studio soft boxes.
